About Me

Final-year Computer Science Engineering student at St. Joseph's College of Engineering and Technology (SJCET), Palai, focused on full-stack development, IoT and embedded systems, and applied AI.

01

Bio

I build scalable, user-centric applications using the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, Node.js), along with Java, Python, SQL and RESTful APIs. Recently, I built a payment-gated web platform integrating Stripe with server-side session verification and webhook-based access control.

Beyond web development, I work with IoT and embedded systems — building sensor-based and automation projects with ESP32 and Arduino. I'm also comfortable producing precise technical documents in LaTeX.

Alongside academics, I take on leadership roles within engineering organizations: Membership Lead of the ASME Student Section, Coordinator of IAM3D under ASME EFX SJCET, and team member on Eonyx, SJCET's EHPVC team under ASME EFX India. I've also served as an Executive Committee Member coordinating events at Asthra, SJCET's national-level tech fest.

I'm graduating soon and looking for Software Engineer or Full-Stack Developer roles where I can build reliable software and keep growing technically.
